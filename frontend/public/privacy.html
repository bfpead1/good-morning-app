Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Good Morning App is a personal morning dashboard used by its owner and a small number of invited family members. Only Google accounts on an approved list can sign in.

What the app accesses

  • Google profile: your name, email address, and profile picture, to sign you in.
  • Google Calendar: your events, to show today's schedule and plan your day. If you allow it, the app can also add or edit events when you ask it to.
  • Gmail (read-only): the sender, subject, and a short snippet of recent emails, to show your inbox and suggest which ones need attention. The app never sends, deletes, or changes email, and never stores email bodies.
  • Canvas LMS (optional): if you connect it, your upcoming and missing assignments. Your Canvas access token is stored encrypted.

How it is used

Data is used only to show your dashboard and generate your daily plan. To build the plan, calendar events, email senders, subjects, and snippets, and assignment titles are sent to Groq's AI service. Nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Your Google sign-in tokens, settings, daily plans, scripture progress, and workout logs are stored in a private Supabase database. The app is hosted on Render.

Google API data

Good Morning App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Use Disconnect Google account in the app's Settings to revoke the app's access and delete your account data. You can also remove access at any time from your Google account permissions.
